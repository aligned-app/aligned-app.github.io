ALIGNVIEW turns an iPad or Mac into a monitor for your camera. Overlay guides and a photo from the last session to get back to the same setup the next day; during interviews, a transcript runs alongside.

Privacy policy

iPad (USB-C, iPadOS 17 or later) / Mac · iPhone over Wi-Fi · Not yet on the App Store

Connecting

Three ways to see the picture

iPad + cameraConnect the camera over USB-C and switch it to USB streaming (UVC) mode. Tested with a Sony a7C II.
Mac + cameraSame USB streaming (UVC). A UVC-compatible HDMI capture card also works as a source.
iPhone viewingiPhone doesn't support external USB cameras, so it watches over Wi-Fi: turn on “Share to Wi-Fi” on the iPad or Mac, and an iPhone on the same network can connect.

Features

What you can overlay

No buttons over the picture; the tools live in the side bar. This list follows the in-app help page; screen recordings are on the way.

GuidesHalves, thirds, golden section, diagonals, Fibonacci spiral, safe area, center cross, circles, and lines you drag in yourself.
Aspect frames9:16, 4:5, 2.39:1 and more, with an optional outside mask.
Onion skinLay a photo semi-transparent over the picture, set the opacity, and hold the eye button to hide it for a quick compare.
Scene memoryEight slots store guides, overlay and doodles together. Load one to get back to the same camera position.
DoodleDraw marks on the picture; undo or clear.
SnapshotSave the clean picture (no guides or doodles) to Photos.
Bypass & clean viewTurn every overlay off in one tap; swipe up to show only the picture.
Interview transcriptText appears live as people talk and is refined after each segment. Flag, annotate and save. The speech model is built into the app and works offline.

Privacy

The picture and transcript stay on your device

Video, snapshots and transcripts are stored only on your device and never uploaded. No account, no tracking. Privacy policy
