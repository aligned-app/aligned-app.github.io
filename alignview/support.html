接相機前想知道的

支援哪些相機？

能輸出 USB 串流（UVC）的相機，或 UVC 規格的 HDMI 採集卡。目前實測過 Sony a7C II。

iPhone 可以直接接相機嗎？

不行。iPhone 不支援外接 USB 相機，相機也不會直接把畫面用 WiFi 傳給 ALIGNVIEW。要先用 iPad 或 Mac 接相機，打開「分享畫面到 WiFi」；再在同一個 WiFi 網路下的 iPhone 開 ALIGNVIEW，允許區域網路權限，從「訊號源」選那台 iPad 或 Mac。

沒有網際網路能用嗎？

能。USB 監看、參考線、疊圖都不需要網際網路，逐字稿的精修也在裝置上完成。例外有兩個：iPhone 收看要跟分享的 iPad／Mac 在同一個區域網路；不支援本機語音辨識的裝置，即時預覽會改用 Apple 的線上服務。

相機要怎麼設定？

把相機的 USB 連線模式切到 USB 串流（UVC），再用 USB-C 線接上 iPad 或 Mac。不同品牌的選單名稱不一樣，請查相機說明書的「USB 串流」或「網路攝影機」。

Before you plug in

Which cameras work?

Any camera that can output USB streaming (UVC), or a UVC-compatible HDMI capture card. Tested so far with a Sony a7C II.

Can an iPhone connect to the camera directly?

No. iPhone doesn't support external USB cameras, and the camera doesn't send its picture to ALIGNVIEW over Wi-Fi. Connect the camera to an iPad or Mac first and turn on “分享畫面到 WiFi” (Share to Wi-Fi). Then open ALIGNVIEW on an iPhone on the same Wi-Fi network, allow local network access, and pick that iPad or Mac under “訊號源” (Source).

Does it work without internet?

Yes. USB monitoring, guides and onion skin need no internet, and transcript refinement runs on the device. Two exceptions: iPhone viewing needs the same local network as the sharing iPad or Mac, and on devices without on-device speech recognition the live preview uses Apple's online service.

How do I set up the camera?

Switch the camera's USB connection mode to USB streaming (UVC), then connect it to the iPad or Mac with a USB-C cable. Menu names differ by brand; look for “USB streaming” or “webcam” in your camera's manual.
